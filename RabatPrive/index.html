The idea

Morocco is not difficult to visit. It is difficult to visit well. Rabat Privé exists for the traveller who would rather spend their time experiencing the country than organising it — a discreet local presence, arranging the details before you arrive and standing beside you while you are here.

How it works

Three steps, no friction

You bring the dates and the intentions. We handle the logistics and the local knowledge.

  1. 01

    Tell us about your trip

    Share your dates, who is travelling, where you would like to go and what you enjoy. A short conversation is usually enough for us to understand the shape of your trip.

  2. 02

    We curate your experience

    We come back with a considered proposal — stays, drivers, tables and experiences — with clear recommendations and honest notes on what is worth your time and what is not.

  3. 03

    Enjoy Morocco

    Once you are here, we help coordinate the details, stay reachable throughout your stay and adjust plans when you would rather do something else entirely.

Marrakech

About

A quiet kind of expertise

Rabat Privé began with a simple observation: the difference between a good trip to Morocco and an unforgettable one is rarely the budget. It is knowing who to call, when to go, and what to skip.

We are a small team with deep roots here. We work with a trusted network of drivers, guides, artisans and hosts we have known for years, and we take on a limited number of travellers at a time so that every request gets proper attention.

Our approach is discreet and unhurried. No scripts, no pressure, no rigid packages. Just clear recommendations, careful coordination, and someone reliable on the other end of the phone while you are here.

  • Local knowledge — first-hand, not researched
  • Discretion — your trip stays yours
  • Attention to detail — the small things matter
  • Honest advice — including what to leave out
